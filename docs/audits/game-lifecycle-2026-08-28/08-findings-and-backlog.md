### Unresolved authorization policy (not ranked as a defect)

Scenario A-007 proves that authenticated Game reads use `authenticate + resolveTenant`, while commands additionally require `games.play`. No frozen contract states whether reads must require `games.play`, a future `games.read`, or ordinary tenant membership. It remains UNKNOWN until the product/security owner supplies the authoritative permission policy; only then can compatibility tests and a severity be assigned.
